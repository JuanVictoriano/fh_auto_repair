About the shop

Serving our community since 1993 — six bays on Hemlock Street, the same family working in them, and one rule that hasn't changed: nobody gets a bill they didn't agree to.

Who we are

An independent shop, run by the people turning the wrenches

F&H opened in 1993 in a garage that had already been fixing cars in Cypress Hills for a generation before that. We kept the building, replaced the equipment, and built the business on repeat customers — the kind who send their sister, their neighbor, and eventually their kid with a first car.

We're not a chain, and there's no service advisor working on commission between you and the technician. When you call, you're talking to somebody who has been under your car or can walk over and look at it.

Most of what comes through the door is ordinary: brakes, batteries, inspections, check engine lights, the noise that started last Tuesday. We handle the complicated jobs too — timing components, A/C systems, electrical faults that other shops gave up on — but we're not interested in selling you work your car doesn't need to get through another winter.

How we work

Four promises we actually keep

The estimate comes first

Parts and labor in writing before the job starts. If the number changes, the work stops until we've called you and you've said yes.

You keep your old parts

Ask and we'll box up whatever we replaced. If a part didn't need replacing, you'd know — so we don't replace parts that don't need it.

We'll tell you to wait

Some things can safely go another six months. When that's true, we say so and write it on the invoice for next time.

The work is warrantied

24 months or 24,000 miles on parts and labor, whichever comes first. If something we fixed comes back, bring it back.

A repair you understand is a repair you can trust.
