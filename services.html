Services & Pricing

What the common jobs run at our shop. Every figure below covers parts and labor for most passenger cars and light trucks. You'll get an exact number in writing before we start.

How to read these numbers Ranges reflect the difference between a four-cylinder commuter and a loaded SUV, and between economy and OE-grade parts. Sales tax and shop supplies are not included. European models, hybrids, and diesels often fall above the top of a range — call and we'll price your exact vehicle.
