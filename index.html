How a repair works here

You approve the price before we start

  1. Step 01

    Drop it off and tell us what it's doing

    Noise, smell, warning light, or just a feeling something's off — describe it in your own words. No appointment needed for a look.

  2. Step 02

    We diagnose it and call you with a written estimate

    Parts and labor, itemized, before anything is taken apart. If we find something else along the way, you get another call.

  3. Step 03

    You say go, we fix it and road-test it

    Every car gets driven before it's handed back. You pay at the counter when you pick it up — cash, card, or check.
